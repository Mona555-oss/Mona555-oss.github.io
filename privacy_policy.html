1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect and store the information necessary to operate the App.

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Full name

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Allow you to purchase lesson packages.
  • Schedule, reschedule, and cancel lessons.
  • Track remaining lessons within purchased packages.
  • Send account verification, booking confirmations, reminders, and other service-related notifications.
  • Maintain the reliability, security, and functionality of the App.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We use trusted third-party providers to operate the App.

Supabase

Supabase provides:

  • User authentication
  • Secure cloud database storage
  • Backend infrastructure

Your account information and booking data are securely stored and processed using Supabase.

Supabase uses encrypted communication (TLS/SSL) and industry-standard security practices to help protect your information.

Bookitit

Bookitit is used exclusively to manage lesson scheduling and calendar availability.

The Bookitit interface is not visible to users. All booking operations are performed through the App.

Only the information necessary to create and manage appointments is shared with Bookitit, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Appointment details

No unnecessary personal information is shared.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.
