Zeituna — Delete Your Account

If you would like to permanently delete your Zeituna account and associated data, you can request account deletion using the instructions below.

What will be deleted?

When your deletion request is submitted:

  • Your Zeituna account will be scheduled for permanent deletion.
  • Your personal account information will be deleted.
  • Your booking data will be removed.
  • Your unused lessons and packages associated with the account will no longer be accessible.
Account deletion is permanent.

Your account will be permanently deleted within 3 business days after your deletion request.

What happens after I request deletion?

If you log in to your account before the permanent deletion is completed, your account may be reactivated.

However, booking data that has already been removed immediately after the deletion request cannot be recovered.

Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Zeituna account, send an email from the email address associated with your Zeituna account.

Email: alfleyla.dev@outlook.com

Subject: Zeituna Account Deletion Request

We will process your account deletion request within 3 business days.
